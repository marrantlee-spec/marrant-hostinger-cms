## Product specification tables

For new bilingual product pages, use the approved Q1002 specification-table structure by default: omit Product Category and Weight; use concise labels such as `Size` and `Color`; combine MOQ, packaging, and production lead time into one field with `Contact us for details` / `具体请联系我们咨询`; and keep the compact paired-row layout without empty cells. Only deviate when the user explicitly requests a product-specific exception.

Keep evidence provenance in internal records, not customer-facing product copy. Avoid phrases such as `所提供资料`, `图片展示`, `supplied product sheet`, or `the images show`; describe the product directly in both languages. Label the current color section `Existing SKU Colors` / `现有SKU颜色`.
