# Marrant Weekly SEO / GEO / AIO Content Plan

**Planning week:** September 15–20, 2026  
**Audience:** International importers, distributors, e-commerce brands, private-label buyers and product developers sourcing genuine leather bags and small leather goods  
**Business goal:** Turn research-stage visitors into qualified RFQs while building topical authority around OEM/ODM development, quality control and buyer preparation

## Research Basis

The site currently presents Marrant as a Guangzhou OEM/ODM manufacturing partner for leather bags, wallets and small leather goods. Its strongest verifiable experience signals are development support from concept to sample; material inspection before cutting; cutting, stitching, structure, hardware and accessory checks; finished-product inspection; packing review; buyer visits; and shipment coordination.

Existing English articles already target:

- choosing a leather bag manufacturer in China;
- choosing a full-grain leather wallet; and
- sourcing a Crazy Horse leather travel tote.

The new cluster avoids rewriting those primary intents. It answers adjacent, higher-conversion questions a buyer asks between supplier discovery and inquiry.

Google’s current guidance says that normal SEO foundations also apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode: pages need to be indexable and eligible for snippets, and content should be helpful, reliable and people-first. Google also describes query fan-out for complex comparisons. The content plan therefore uses direct answer blocks, explicit decision factors, tables, product-specific examples, operational checklists and tightly scoped FAQs rather than producing many thin pages for query variants.

No search-volume figures are claimed in this plan because the workspace does not include Google Search Console, Google Ads Keyword Planner or a paid keyword database. Topic selection is based on visible site positioning, existing-page gap analysis, live SERP language and commercial intent. Validate query demand after publication with Search Console impressions and qualified inquiry data.

### Recommended on-page implementation

- Render the article body server-side and provide a self-referencing canonical.
- Add `BlogPosting` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD with accurate author/publisher, publication date, modified date and hero image.
- Keep the FAQ visible in the article. `FAQPage` markup may describe the content when eligible, but do not expect a Google FAQ rich result; markup should match visible text exactly.
- Use one descriptive H1. Preserve question-form H2/H3 headings where they match buyer intent.
- Add an author link to a real Marrant team or editorial-policy page. Do not invent an individual expert profile.
- Include a short “Reviewed by Marrant Product Development Team” line only if the team actually reviews and approves the article.
- Give all generated or factory images a unique filename, width/height, responsive sizes and descriptive ALT text. Do not place keyword lists in ALT attributes.
- Index the articles through the XML sitemap and add them to the English blog listing.

## 5. CTA and Popup Plan

Each article includes:

- one contextual mid-article CTA after the reader has received a useful decision framework;
- one final CTA after the conclusion;
- a popup title, supporting copy, triggers, fields, submit label and trust note.

Global rules:

- Required fields: **Name, Email and Phone**, per the brief.
- Recommended qualification fields: Company, Country/Region, Product Type, Estimated Quantity and Message; enable file upload for RFQ/specification documents.
- On mobile, do not use exit intent. Trigger after 40% scroll and 30 seconds only when the visitor has not opened another inquiry form.
- Limit the popup to once per session and suppress it for 14 days after dismissal or permanently after a successful inquiry.
- Preserve article position after the form closes and make the modal keyboard accessible.
- Track `cta_mid_click`, `cta_end_click`, `popup_view`, `popup_start`, `inquiry_submit` and `qualified_inquiry` by article slug. Optimize for qualified inquiries, not raw form opens.

## Editorial and Evidence Notes

- The drafts avoid unsupported claims about certifications, years in business, production capacity, customer counts, defect rates, fixed MOQ and fixed lead time.
- Each article includes a short Marrant advantage section based only on site-verifiable operations: Guangzhou showroom/production access, product-development and sampling support, staged production checks, flexible customization, and shipment preparation.
- References to testing are written as recommended, defined procedures—not claims that a specific test was passed.
- ISO acceptance sampling is described conceptually. The article does not prescribe a universal AQL value and directs buyers to select the applicable current standard and plan with qualified inspectors.
- Leather traceability or sustainability claims should be added only when Marrant can provide product-specific supply-chain evidence. A tannery’s certification should not be presented as the bag factory’s certification.
- Replace illustrative AI factory images with authentic Marrant images wherever the claimed process is actually documented.
